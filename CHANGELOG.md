## [Initial Public Release] — 2026

### Added

- Country-specific name-day calendars
- Search by name, date and country
- Favorites, tracked countries and upcoming celebrations
- Daily local reminders and home-screen widget
- Premium Lifetime and optional Supporter products
- Eligible alternative launcher icons
- No advertising SDKs or behavioral tracking

### Documentation

- Added README, Privacy Policy, Terms of Use, Support and Security documentation.
- Added standardized GitHub issue and pull-request templates.

For the current production release, visit [Name Days on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.namedaysdc).
