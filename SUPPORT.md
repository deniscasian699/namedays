## 🔧 Common Issues & Solutions

### 🗓️ Missing or Incorrect Name Day

Check the selected country and date. Traditions can vary by region, denomination
and source. Send the country, name, date and a reliable source with your report.

### 📱 Widgets Not Refreshing

1. Open Name Days and check your selected country.
2. Check Android's background and battery restrictions for the app.
3. Remove and re-add the widget if your launcher keeps an old copy.

Customize all widgets from **Settings → Customize home screen widget**.
The widget picker uses a static preview on older Android versions and a layout
preview on newer ones. Actual appearance depends on your launcher.

### 🔔 Notifications Not Arriving

1. Enable reminders in Name Days Settings.
2. Allow notifications in Android settings.
3. Check Do Not Disturb and battery restrictions.
4. Follow the reminder-reliability options offered by the app.

### 💎 Purchase Not Activating

1. Use the Google Play account used for the purchase.
2. Connect to the internet and choose **Restore Purchases** in Settings.
3. Reopen the app to refresh purchase status.
4. Contact support with your Google Play Order ID if the issue persists.

### ❤️ Subscription Still Active After a One-Time Purchase

A one-time purchase does not cancel a separate Monthly Supporter subscription.
Manage it yourself through [Google Play subscriptions](https://play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions).
Cancellation normally stops renewal while benefits continue until expiration.

### 🔔 How to Remove Ads

Choose **One-Time Supporter** or **Monthly Supporter**.
One-Time Supporter removes ads while its entitlement remains valid; Monthly
Supporter removes ads while active. **Premium Lifetime does not remove ads**;
it unlocks unlimited tracked countries and favorites, plus custom reminder time.

Ads may return after a refund, revocation or subscription expiration once the
updated status is verified. Neither Supporter product unlocks Premium limits.

### 🛡️ Advertising Privacy Choices

Where required, **Settings → Privacy options (GDPR / ads)** opens Google's UMP
privacy-options form. It is hidden while ad removal is active. Declining
personalization does not necessarily remove non-personalized or limited ads.
Android privacy settings also provide advertising-ID controls.
