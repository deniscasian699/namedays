## Common Questions

### Reminders do not arrive

Allow notifications, keep reminders enabled, and review the device’s battery restrictions. Android cannot guarantee an exact minute on every device.

### A name or date looks wrong

Name-day traditions differ by source and region. Open a cultural-data issue with the country, name, expected date and a reliable reference.

### Premium did not restore

Use Restore Purchases while signed in to the same Google account that made the purchase.

## Billing and Refunds

Billing is handled by Google Play. Manage subscriptions at [Google Play Subscriptions](https://play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions). Refund requests are governed by [Google Play’s refund policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637).

Never post payment details, full order receipts, private QR content, reminder text, travel notes or other sensitive information in a public issue.
